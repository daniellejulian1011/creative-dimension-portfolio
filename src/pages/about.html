I'm Danielle Julian, a multidisciplinary designer working across graphic design, UX/UI, branding, advertising, web design, and interaction design.

I turn ideas into clear visual systems through research, wireframes, typography, brand identity, polished interfaces, and expressive storytelling. Berry Vibes Studio is the name I give that practice.

Design philosophy

Playful systems. Purposeful stories.

Designing playful systems with purpose means pairing warmth with logic — a cute app with serious product logic, and visual work with real narrative intent.

I start with research before polish — audience statements, notes, personas and low-fidelity flows — then move into visual systems, interface decisions, code and presentation.
